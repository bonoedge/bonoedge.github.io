bonoedge Content Tools

bonoedge makes short documentary and story videos for its own YouTube channels in Korean, English, Spanish and Japanese.

bonoedge Content Tools is the internal app behind that work. It has two jobs:

  • Publish — it uploads finished videos to our own channels as private or scheduled videos (YouTube Data API).
  • Measure — it reads each channel's own performance reports, such as average view duration and subscribers gained, to learn which videos work (YouTube Analytics API).

The app only connects to channels owned by bonoedge. It is not offered to other users.
